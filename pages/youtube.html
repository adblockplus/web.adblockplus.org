{{ youtube__faq-1-question Does Adblock Plus still work on YouTube? }}

{{ youtube__faq-1-answer Yes. Adblock Plus blocks YouTube ads, including pre-roll, mid-roll, post-roll, banner and overlay ads. Our team updates Adblock Plus regularly to keep pace with changes YouTube makes to how it delivers ads, so you stay covered. }}

{{ youtube__faq-2-question What types of YouTube ads does Adblock Plus block? }}

{{ youtube__faq-2-answer Adblock Plus blocks the main YouTube ad formats: skippable and unskippable video ads, bumper ads, overlay ads and sponsored cards. It also removes the banner and sidebar ads that appear next to videos. }}

{{ youtube__faq-3-question Does Adblock Plus make YouTube faster? }}

{{ youtube__faq-3-answer Yes. Adblock Plus stops ads and the tracking scripts that come with them before they load, so your browser has less to process on each page. Videos start sooner and playlists play through without ad breaks. }}

{{ youtube__faq-4-question Is Adblock Plus free for YouTube? }}

{{ youtube__faq-4-answer Yes. Adblock Plus is free to download and use, and YouTube ad blocking is included in the free version. Adblock Plus Premium adds extra features on top, including cookie banner blocking. }}

{{ youtube__faq-5-question Are YouTube ads part of Acceptable Ads? }}

{{ youtube__faq-5-answer No. Video ads do not meet the Acceptable Ads standard, so YouTube's pre-roll, mid-roll and post-roll ads can never be on the Acceptable Ads allowlist. Adblock Plus blocks them whether Acceptable Ads is turned on or off. Learn more about Acceptable Ads. }}

{{ youtube__faq-6-question Why does YouTube sometimes show me a message about my ad blocker? }}

{{ youtube__faq-6-answer YouTube regularly changes its site to detect ad blockers, and our team updates Adblock Plus to keep up. If you see a message asking you to turn off your ad blocker, make sure Adblock Plus is up to date and reload the page. If the message continues, you can choose to allow ads on YouTube once or always. Adblock Plus keeps blocking ads on every other site. }}
